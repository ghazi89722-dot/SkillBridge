# SkillBridge — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Overview
- **Product name:** SkillBridge
- **One-liner:** An academia–industry skill readiness platform that assesses what students actually know, closes the gap against real role requirements, verifies improvement, and connects them to the right opportunity — with AYUSH as the flagship domain.
- **Hackathon:** Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement **SIH26044**
- **Sponsoring Organization:** Ministry of Ayush (Department: All India Institute of Ayurveda — AIIA)
- **Category / Theme:** Software / Smart Automation

## 2. Problem Statement (official, condensed)
A significant gap exists between the skills students acquire in academic institutions and the competencies industry expects. Students don't know precisely what they're missing; industry can't verify self-declared resume skills; institutions and the Ministry lack visibility into systemic, cross-college skill gaps needed to fix curricula and target training investment.

## 3. Positioning
SkillBridge is **not a job board**. Traditional platforms run `PROFILE → SEARCH → APPLY`. SkillBridge runs:

```
CLAIM → ASSESS → ANALYSE GAP → LEARN → REASSESS → VERIFY → MATCH → APPLY
```

The platform is **domain-agnostic infrastructure**: the skill taxonomy, assessment engine, and matching engine are built to work for any field. **AYUSH is our flagship domain implementation** — the competency taxonomy is designed to support AYUSH-specific skills and curriculum references. This is *not* presented as an official, ministry-sourced curriculum feed unless that integration is actually built and verified.

## 4. Goals
- **G1** — Give every student an honest, evidence-based readiness picture for a specific role/internship, not a self-declared resume.
- **G2** — Give industry an explainable, trustworthy shortlist instead of a pile of unverifiable claims.
- **G3** — Give institutions and the Ministry/NCISM an aggregated, real-time view of systemic skill gaps to guide curriculum and training decisions.
- **G4** — Ship a working, demoable MVP within the hackathon window with no fake-AI theater — every score must be explainable and deterministic.

## 5. Non-goals (MVP)
- Not a general social/networking feed.
- Not a full LMS — MVP links out to curated external learning resources rather than hosting courses.
- Not blockchain-anchored credentials — schema is designed for it, not built in MVP.
- Not an official AIIA/NCISM live curriculum feed — MVP uses a clearly labeled, manually curated AYUSH sample dataset.

## 6. Personas
| Persona | Core need |
|---|---|
| **Student** (e.g. BAMS/BHMS undergrad) | Know real readiness, close gaps, get a fair shot regardless of college tier or location |
| **Industry recruiter** (pharma, Panchakarma center, wellness startup, hospital) | Fast, trustworthy, explainable shortlist |
| **Institution admin** | Visibility into where students fall behind; direct targeted training |
| **Academician / Faculty** | Discover industry collaboration — FDPs, guest lectures, joint research, mentorship |
| **Ministry / NCISM viewer** | National, aggregated, cross-institution skill-gap visibility |

## 7. Core features by role
- **Student:** claim skills → take assessment → view skill gap → get learning recommendation → reassess → verified skill → digital skill passport → explainable opportunity matches → apply → track application.
- **Industry:** post opportunity with required skills + minimum proficiency thresholds → view ranked, explainable candidate shortlist → shortlist/reject → give post-internship feedback.
- **Institution:** aggregate student readiness dashboard → skill-gap trend view → manage training programs.
- **Academician:** discover/request industry collaboration (FDP, guest lecture, joint research, mentorship, live projects).
- **Ministry/NCISM:** read-only, cross-institution aggregated skill-gap heatmap.

## 8. Success metrics (hackathon demo + pitch)
- The full lifecycle works live, end to end: claim → assess → gap → learn → reassess → verified → matched → applied.
- Every score shown anywhere in the product is explainable — a breakdown, never a bare number.
- At least one complete AYUSH-specific example role (e.g. "Panchakarma Therapist Intern") demoed with realistic skill thresholds and a believable before/after reassessment story.

## 9. Scope by phase
- **MVP (hackathon):** single flagship domain (AYUSH sample data), full student/industry loop, domain-agnostic schema underneath.
- **Phase 2:** Institution + Ministry dashboards, academician collaboration layer, micro-internships.
- **Phase 3:** Tele-internship credit workflow, DigiLocker/NAD verification integration, animated 3D homepage polish, additional domains beyond AYUSH.

## 10. Risks
- Overclaiming curriculum sourcing (must stay factually accurate — see Rules.md).
- Assessment gaming / low-effort answers inflating scores (see security.md).
- Scope creep beyond the MVP loop before the core story is solid.
